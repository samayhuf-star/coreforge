# Privacy Policy for CoreForge

Last updated: November 11, 2026

## Introduction
CoreForge ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the CoreForge mobile application.

## Information We Collect
- **Account information**: If you create an account (email, optional profile details).
- **Workout data**: Progress, streaks, selected plans, and custom workouts (stored locally on your device by default).
- **Device information**: Basic app usage for improving performance (crash reports if enabled).
- **Purchase information**: Handled by Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not store full payment details.

## How We Use Information
- To provide and improve the app
- To track your workouts and progress
- To process subscriptions via the respective app stores
- To communicate support responses if you contact us

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with:
- Apple / Google for in-app purchases and analytics (as per their policies)
- Service providers strictly for app functionality (e.g., crash reporting)

## Data Storage
Most progress data stays on your device. If cloud sync is added later, it will be optional and encrypted.

## Your Rights
You can request deletion of any account data by contacting support.

## Contact
support@coreforge.app or open an issue on this repository.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use means acceptance of changes.
