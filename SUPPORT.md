# CoreForge Support

## Contact
For support, feature requests, or bug reports:
- Email: support@coreforge.app
- Or open an issue in this repository: https://github.com/samayhuf-star/coreforge/issues

## Common Questions
**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Manage subscriptions in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes. The basic Beginner plan is available for free. Pro unlocks all plans, the custom generator, and full progress features.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes, core workouts and progress tracking work offline.

**How do I restore purchases?**
Use the Restore Purchase option on the paywall screen.

## Response Time
We aim to reply within 48 hours on business days.
